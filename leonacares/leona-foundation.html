Leona Foundation

Leona Foundation exists to keep families housed by providing free emergency moving assistance, furniture donations, and cleanouts for those facing housing instability. We connect community resources and partner companies to make sure families aren’t left without support when it matters most.

What we provide

  • Free emergency moves for families facing eviction, domestic violence, or unsafe living conditions.
  • Furniture and essential household items to help families rebuild safe, stable homes.
  • Cleanouts and junk removal in crisis situations where a safe reset is needed.
  • Warm, judgment-free support focused on dignity and stability.

How the partnership works

The Foundation works hand-in-hand with Precise Movers LLC and Happy to Help LLC. When community members or businesses book paid moves, junk removal, or property preservation services, a portion of that work supports free crisis moves and household essentials for families who cannot afford them.
