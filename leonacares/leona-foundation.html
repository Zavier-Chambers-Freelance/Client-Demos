Leona Foundation

The Leona Foundation exists to make sure families facing crisis are never left alone. We provide free emergency moving services, donated furniture and essentials, and temporary housing support for those experiencing eviction, unsafe living conditions, or sudden hardship.

What We Provide

  • Free emergency moves for families in eviction, domestic violence, or unsafe environments.
  • Furniture donations and essentials to help rebuild safe homes after crisis.
  • Cleanouts and reset assistance for households overwhelmed by sudden hardship.
  • Warm, judgment-free support focused on dignity and stability.

How Our Partnership Model Works

The Leona Foundation partners with Precise Movers and H2H Property Preservation & Junk Removal.

When the community books paid moving, cleanout, or property preservation services, a portion of that revenue helps fund free crisis moves and essential support for families who cannot afford them.
