Welcome to the Leona Foundation — We Care, We Move, We Support.

At Leona Cares, we believe no family should face crisis alone. Our mission is to provide free emergency move assistance, furniture donations, and temporary housing support to families experiencing eviction, foreclosure, unsafe living conditions, or sudden hardship.

We step in when families need us most — helping them relocate, stabilize, and rebuild with dignity, safety, and compassion.

💙 What We Do

✔ Emergency Crisis Moves (Free)

We provide urgent relocation assistance for families facing:

  • Eviction or foreclosure
  • Unsafe or unstable homes
  • Loss of income
  • Domestic or family-related crises

✔ Donated Furniture & Home Essentials

We collect and redistribute gently-used furniture and essentials to help families rebuild their homes after crisis.

✔ Temporary Housing Support

We work to ensure families have safe, short-term living options during emergency transitions.

🤝 Partnered Services (Affordable Options)

To support our mission, we partner with two trusted local companies that provide affordable community services.

🚛 Precise Movers

Professional, reliable moving services for local and long-distance relocations.

Free for qualifying hardship cases through our foundation.
