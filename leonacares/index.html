Helping Families Move Forward

The Leona Foundation exists for the moments when life changes without warning. We step in with free move assistance, furniture donations, and housing support for families facing eviction, foreclosure, or unstable living conditions.

When families must start over, we help them move forward with dignity, safety, and hope.

How We Make A Difference

Through emergency move assistance, home essentials, and transitional housing support, we walk alongside families during some of the hardest transitions of their lives.

Emergency Move Assistance

Urgent relocation support when families must move quickly due to crisis.

  • Eviction or foreclosure moves
  • Support during unsafe or unstable living conditions
  • Help after sudden loss of income or crisis events
Home Essentials Program

Basic items that help turn empty spaces into safe, comfortable homes.

  • Furnishings for families starting over
  • Basic household essentials provided
  • Helps turn empty spaces into homes
Transitional Housing Assistance

Short-term housing options while families work toward stability.

  • Short-term housing options for families in crisis
  • Support while working toward stable, permanent housing
  • Safe temporary placement during major life transitions
