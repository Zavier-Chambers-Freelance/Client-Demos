H2H Property Preservation & Junk Removal

H2H provides junk removal, property cleanouts, and preservation services for homeowners, landlords, investors, and property managers.

Our Services

  • Whole-house cleanouts / trashouts
  • Eviction cleanouts & turnover preparation
  • Donation recovery (usable items go to families in need)
  • Basic lawn care & exterior cleanup
  • Property preservation for owners & asset managers
