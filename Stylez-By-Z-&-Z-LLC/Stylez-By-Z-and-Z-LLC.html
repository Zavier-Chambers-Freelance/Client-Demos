• I take pictures of my work, so please come picture ready.
• Lashes are available – I have strip lashes for $10.

• For any add-ons such as color, length, bohemian/BOHO look, or 30 inch hair, please use the “add on” option in the booking system and add it to your service.
• Services that include color hair, 30 inches or longer, Bohemian/Boho, or any loc extension service need to be booked 2–3 days before your appointment day.

• Sista Loc services require a consultation appointment before your service appointment.
• If you need locz reattached, the fee is for each single loc.
• If I am not the stylist who regularly maintains your locz and you need some fixed, you will need to schedule a full retwist service.

• There is a 15 minute grace period from the time of your appointment.
  After that, the appointment will automatically cancel unless we discussed otherwise.

• I give one courtesy reschedule with your deposit, discussed with me only.
• All reschedules need to be discussed with me before the reschedule is made in the system.

• Any service that is 2 hours or longer needs to be scheduled before 3–4pm unless we discuss otherwise.
• If you are running late, you must communicate with me first. You cannot just tell me you will be late if it affects other appointments.

• Anyone booking a same-day or squeeze-in spot will have a $30 fee added.

• All plats, locz, or twist styles need morning appointments only.
• Any school-age child during weekdays will need a morning appointment if getting braids.
• The only time I will take an afternoon weekday appointment for a child is if it’s a quick style: blow out, non-braid ponytail, natural style, or quick feed-in style.

• Only the person receiving the service should stay, other than a parent staying with a child.
• Please do not bring extra guests to your appointment.
• Any child receiving service is OK — but please do not bring other children who are not getting service.

• If you do not already have my address, it will be texted to the phone number attached to your appointment.
• Make sure your phone number is correct when you book.
• You can always text 412-530-5009 if you have questions about the address or your booking.
